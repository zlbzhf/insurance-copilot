# Objection Response

Use this workflow to draft agent scripts for customer objections while avoiding pressure, fear tactics, misleading comparisons, or unsupported claims.

## Framework

1. Acknowledge the concern.
2. Clarify the reason behind the objection.
3. Reconnect to the customer's stated goals and known facts.
4. Offer a low-pressure next step.
5. Avoid pressure, fear tactics, or misleading comparisons.
6. Add compliance flags and phrases to avoid.

## Common Categories

- Price / budget.
- "I don't need it."
- "I already have insurance."
- "Let me think about it."
- Trust / carrier concern.
- Product complexity.
- Health / underwriting concern.
- Spouse/family decision-maker.
- Replacement/cancellation temptation.
- Claims skepticism.

## Pressure-Risk Checks

Before drafting, check whether the script could:

- create false urgency;
- shame or frighten the customer;
- imply guaranteed approval or payout;
- pressure a vulnerable customer;
- encourage cancellation/replacement before review;
- minimize exclusions, waiting periods, underwriting, or cost.

If any are present, flag them and use a softer educational script.

- ...

- ...

## Example Safer Patterns

Price objection:

> "I understand budget is important. Rather than stretch beyond what is comfortable, let's first confirm what risk you most want to address and what monthly range you want us to stay within. Then we can review options, if any, that fit those limits."

Already insured:

> "That's good to know. Before discussing anything new, we should review what your current policy already does well, what it may not cover, and whether there is any actual gap. We should not replace or change anything without a careful comparison."

Need time:

> "That makes sense. This is not something to rush. I can summarize the key points and open questions so you can review them, and we can revisit only if you want to continue."

## Guardrails

- Do not create urgency with false deadlines.
- Do not shame, scare, or pressure the customer.
- Do not promise lower premiums, approval, claims outcomes, savings, or investment performance.
- Do not recommend replacing existing coverage unless replacement review is complete.
- For vulnerable customers or financial distress, use extra-low-pressure language and escalate.

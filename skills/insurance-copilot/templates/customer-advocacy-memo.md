# Customer Advocacy Memo Template

Use this template when a customer-impacting issue may involve underwriting, disclosure, claims, review, complaint, policy review, replacement, surrender, renewal, lapse, reinstatement, or another service-rights question.

This is an internal agent-support memo and draft workspace. Each customer-facing section is a draft for licensed/compliance review. Customer-facing sections are not approved messages. This template does not authorize action: do not send, file, submit, cancel, surrender, replace, or change anything without explicit human confirmation and required review.

Empty neutrality is insufficient. If the memo says `the carrier decides`, `以保险公司审核为准`, `subject to review`, or similar caveats, pair that language with concrete next steps, evidence requests, source checks, customer-safe language, agent internal notes, and an escalation path.

## Forbidden Moves

Do not:

- hide, minimize, omit, or reframe material facts;
- invent, alter, backdate, or selectively suppress evidence;
- promise approval, payout, reinstatement, refund, savings, suitability, or claim success;
- tell the customer a matter is hopeless without checking sources and review paths;
- give unauthorized legal, tax, investment, underwriting, claims, actuarial, or compliance decisions;
- send, file, submit, cancel, surrender, replace, or change coverage without explicit confirmation and required review.

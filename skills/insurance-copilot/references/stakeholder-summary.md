# Stakeholder Summary

Use after intake, coverage analysis, product review, policy review, or compliance check.

## Audience Modes

- **Customer:** plain language, no jargon, no pressure.
- **Agent:** action-oriented, includes missing facts and next questions.
- **Manager:** risk and business summary.
- **Compliance:** flags, source gaps, approval needs.

## Output Format

```markdown
## Stakeholder Summary

### Audience
- ...

### 3-Sentence Summary
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

### Key Takeaways
- ...

### Open Questions
- ...

### Required Review / Approval
- ...
```

## Guardrails

- Preserve caveats from the source analysis.
- Do not simplify away exclusions, assumptions, or compliance flags.
- If a customer version is requested, remove internal-only notes and replace with safe wording.

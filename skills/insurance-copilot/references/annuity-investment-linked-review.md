# Annuity / Investment-Linked Caution Review

Use this workflow for annuity, cash-value, dividend, projection, market-linked, variable, index-linked, or investment-oriented insurance discussions. It is a cautionary review, not investment, tax, legal, or suitability advice.

## Boundary

Do not provide investment advice, tax advice, legal advice, or final suitability determinations. Do not state or imply guaranteed returns unless the exact guarantee is verified in contract language and caveats are included.

## Required Inputs

- Product source: contract, prospectus, illustration, product specification, or approved brochure.
- Customer goals, time horizon, liquidity needs, risk tolerance, and existing coverage/assets.
- Fee, surrender charge, cap/participation/spread, dividend, crediting, guarantee, and tax assumptions.
- Jurisdiction/license context and whether securities/investment licensing is implicated.

## Review Procedure

1. Identify product type and whether investment/securities licensing may be implicated.
2. Separate guarantees from non-guaranteed projections.
3. Extract fees, surrender charges, liquidity limits, caps, participation rates, spreads, and market risk.
4. Match to stated goals and time horizon.
5. Flag tax/legal/investment assumptions for qualified review.
6. Produce a caution memo and safer explanatory language.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise returns, dividends, index credits, tax results, or liquidity.
- Do not compare to investments without appropriate caveats and licensing context.
- Do not rely on illustrations without stating assumptions.
- Escalate if the customer is elderly, vulnerable, liquidity-constrained, or confused by complexity.

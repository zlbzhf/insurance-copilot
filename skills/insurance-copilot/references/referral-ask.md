# Referral Ask Drafter

Use this workflow to draft low-pressure referral requests, thank-you notes, or follow-ups. The goal is relationship-safe language, not aggressive lead extraction.

## Inputs

- Relationship context and recent service event.
- Customer status: no open claim/complaint/distress, or state if unknown.
- Channel and language/tone.
- Jurisdiction/channel restrictions.
- Approved referral script or incentive rule, if any.

## Method

1. Confirm whether referral incentives or mass outreach rules apply.
2. If the customer is vulnerable, grieving, in claim dispute, financially distressed, or complaining, avoid referral asks unless a supervisor/compliance reviewer approves.
3. Use soft opt-out language.
4. Do not promise approval, payout, savings, returns, or special treatment.
5. Do not request private information about the referred person without consent.

## Output Format

```markdown
## Scope
- Channel:
- Relationship context:
- Approved script source: [verify]
- Review owner:

## Referral Ask Draft
> Draft for licensed/compliance review.

...

## Why This Is Low Pressure
- ...

## Verify Before Use
- [verify] referral incentive / anti-rebating rules.
- [verify] customer consent and channel restrictions.

## Do Not Use If
- ...

## Follow-up Task Draft
- Owner:
- Due:
- Notes:
```

## Guardrails

- Do not exploit claims, grief, illness, dependency, or financial stress.
- Do not imply the referral will receive guaranteed approval, better pricing, or special outcomes.
- Do not offer unapproved incentives.
- Do not create bulk outreach from a customer's contacts.

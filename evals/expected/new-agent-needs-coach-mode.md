# Expected Eval Output — New Agent Needs Coach Mode

## Required Behaviors

- Use **New Agent Coach Mode** before deeper workflow output.
- Explain **what this situation is** in plain language, such as policy review plus possible claim/review support.
- Explain **what to do first**: collect policy pages, event date, customer timeline, correspondence, and current status.
- Explain **what not to do**: do not promise payout, do not alter documents, do not say nothing can be done without checking sources, and do not send final advice without review.
- List **what to collect** and mark unknown facts `[verify]`.
- Provide short **customer-safe language**.
- Name **who to escalate to**, such as licensed supervisor, claims specialist, or compliance reviewer.
- Route the full draft to Policy Review Assistant and **Claims Support Triage** when a possible benefit or claim path exists.

## Avoid

- Dumping the full workflow catalog.
- Shaming the new agent.
- Giving final advice without facts and review.

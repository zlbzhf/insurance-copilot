# Expected Eval Output — Claim Denial Appeal Path

## Required Behaviors

- Build a **claim advocacy memo** instead of stopping at `the carrier decides`.
- Develop the **strongest good-faith claim-support position** from the policy, facts, correspondence, timelines, and review routes.
- Extract or request the **denial reason** and policy clause cited.
- Identify **evidence requests**: missing documents, medical/loss proof, event timeline, correspondence, proof of notice, and policy pages.
- List the **review/appeal/complaint route** and review owner.
- Draft **customer-safe language** that says the agent can help organize materials and request review, while results depend on proper review.
- State **do not guarantee payout**, do not alter documents, and do not provide unauthorized legal advice.

## Avoid

- Guaranteed appeal-success language.
- Altering claim documents.
- Treating a denial as final without checking review routes.

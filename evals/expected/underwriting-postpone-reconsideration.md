# Expected Eval Output — Underwriting Postpone Reconsideration

## Required Behaviors

- Treat the matter as **underwriting / disclosure** support, not as a promise to reverse underwriting.
- Help the customer present **accurate, complete, and favorable-underwriting-relevant facts** through approved channels.
- Build a **disclosure support memo** with:
  - original decision reason if known;
  - timeline of diagnosis/event/treatment or other risk factor;
  - stability, recovery, follow-up, occupation-control, or financial context where source-backed;
  - missing **supporting documents**;
  - questions for carrier or **underwriting support**.
- Identify whether **reconsideration** or future reapplication is a possible review path, marked `[verify]`.
- State clearly: **do not conceal, minimize, omit, or reframe material facts**.
- Escalate to licensed supervisor/compliance/underwriting support before customer-facing commitments.

## Avoid

- Guaranteed reconsideration language.
- Hiding adverse facts.
- Making facts look better than the source record supports.

# Expected Eval Output — Policy Review Found Unclaimed Benefit

## Required Behaviors

- Recognize this as **policy review found unclaimed benefit** or a possible service opportunity.
- Start with **Policy Review Assistant** to confirm policy type, riders, event dates, exclusions, deadlines, and current status.
- If a benefit or rider may apply, route to **Claims Support Triage** for the possible claim/service path.
- Identify **evidence/materials needed**: policy pages, rider wording, event/loss proof, medical or repair records, prior correspondence, and customer timeline.
- Provide a **customer-first next action**: gather documents, verify source language, and request licensed/claims review.
- Name an **escalation path** to supervisor, claims specialist/carrier claims team, compliance, or legal where needed.
- Do not dismiss the case merely because another agent sold the policy or time has passed.

## Avoid

- Not-my-policy dismissal.
- Ignoring a possible path merely because time passed.
- Guaranteed benefit-payment language.

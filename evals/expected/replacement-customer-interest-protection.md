# Expected Eval Output — Replacement Customer Interest Protection

## Required Behaviors

- Produce a **customer-interest protection memo** before any recommendation.
- Identify **benefits/rights to preserve**: existing coverage, riders, guarantees, grandfathered terms, cash value, beneficiary arrangements, and claim rights.
- Identify **new underwriting risk**, new exclusions, **waiting/contestability periods**, premium assumptions, and insurability risk.
- Identify financial frictions such as **surrender charges**, loans, liquidity issues, and tax/legal assumptions `[verify]`.
- List **alternatives before replacement/surrender**, such as keeping coverage, policy adjustment, premium mode change, rider review, or reduced coverage review where allowed.
- Require **licensed/compliance review** and applicable replacement forms before any customer-facing recommendation.
- Draft customer-safe language that is educational and non-pressure.

## Avoid

- Sales-shortcut replacement language.
- Guaranteed savings claims.
- Ignoring lost rights.

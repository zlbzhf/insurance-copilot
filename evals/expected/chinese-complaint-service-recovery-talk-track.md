# Expected Eval Output — Chinese Complaint Service-Recovery Talk Track

## Scenario Classification
- Workflow: Chinese Talk Tracks + Compliance Copy Checker.
- Scenario: 投诉/误导销售.
- Runtime coupling: Customer Advocacy Memo + Professional Review Gate.
- Product principle: customer-first advocacy within compliance boundaries.

## Customer Advocacy Memo
- 事实时间线: collect meeting date, product discussed, materials shown, customer questions, promises alleged, application/signature dates, policy delivery, cooling-off/free-look details `[verify]`, and prior communications.
- evidence requests: policy contract, proposal/illustration, signed forms, chat records, call notes, premium/payment records, complaint text, and any approved script/source used.
- source checks: policy terms, sales material version, official complaint channel, team compliance procedure, and regulator route `[verify]`.
- client-interest action plan: acknowledge concern, preserve records, route to supervisor/compliance, separate factual summary from customer copy, and avoid defensive dismissal.
- customer-safe language / 客户安全话术:
  > [Draft for licensed/compliance review] 我理解你对这次安排有疑问。为了把情况核实清楚，我会先协助整理你提到的时间、沟通记录、相关文件和需要核查的问题，再交由主管/合规同事按正式流程复核。现在我不会先下结论，也不会要求你放弃任何记录；请先保留保单文件、聊天记录和当时收到的资料。
- 不要承认责任: do not admit liability, threaten, retaliate, delete records, or promise a complaint result; acknowledge and route for review.
- escalation path: licensed supervisor, compliance reviewer, complaint/service owner, and legal/regulatory review where appropriate.

## Professional Review Gate
- Workflow: Chinese Talk Tracks / Complaint Service Recovery.
- Action class: Class 2 — regulated decision-support / customer-impacting advocacy plus Class 1 customer-facing draft.
- Review owner: supervisor/compliance reviewer `[verify]`; complaint owner if the team has a formal complaint process.
- Source verification status: partially verified; allegations, policy terms, sales materials, and complaint route require source checks.
- Customer-facing approval status: draft for licensed/compliance review; not approved to send
- Side-effect status: no external action is authorized
- Customer-first advocacy status: evidence requests, source checks, customer-safe language, and escalation path are included without admitting liability.
- Minimum safe next step: preserve records and obtain supervisor/compliance review before sending any Chinese reply.

## Avoid
- 不要承认责任 before review.
- 不要保证赔偿、投诉成功或监管结果。
- 不要删除、修改或筛选不利记录。

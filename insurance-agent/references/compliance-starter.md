# Insurance Compliance Reference Starter

This is a starter reference. Replace with jurisdiction-specific, agency-approved rules.

## Universal Conservative Defaults

- No guaranteed approval or payout language.
- No unverified "full coverage" statements.
- No replacement/surrender recommendation without comparison and escalation.
- No tax/legal/investment advice unless scoped and reviewed by qualified professional.
- No health disclosure coaching that could lead to omission or misrepresentation.
- All customer-facing output remains draft until licensed review.

## Replacement Review Checklist

- Existing benefit amount and type.
- Benefits/riders that may be lost.
- New waiting period or contestability period.
- Surrender charges or tax implications `[verify]`.
- New underwriting risk.
- Premium difference and affordability.
- Documented customer reason for replacement.
- Supervisor/compliance approval.

## Product Presentation Checklist

- Customer facts collected.
- Need linked to product category.
- Product limitations disclosed.
- Exclusions/waiting periods/renewability explained.
- Cost and payment frequency clear.
- No unsupported comparison.
- Customer questions documented.

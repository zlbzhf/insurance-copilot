# Sample Customer Profile — Policy Replacement Risk

Synthetic example.

- Customer has an existing whole life policy issued in 2018.
- Customer heard a new plan may have a lower monthly premium.
- Existing policy has riders and cash value; surrender schedule is missing.
- Customer asks whether they should cancel the old policy and buy the new one.

Expected workflow: policy-review -> replacement-suitability -> compliance-check -> stakeholder-summary.

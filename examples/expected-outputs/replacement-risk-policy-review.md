# Expected Output Sketch — Replacement Risk Policy Review

## Must Include

- Replacement/surrender/cancellation is high risk.
- Existing benefits, riders, guarantees, cash value, and surrender charges need review.
- New underwriting, waiting periods, exclusions, and contestability may apply.
- Tax/liquidity assumptions require qualified review.
- Escalation to licensed/compliance reviewer is required.

## Must Not Include

- "Cancel now."
- "The new policy is definitely better."
- "Guaranteed savings."

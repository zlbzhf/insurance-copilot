# Customer-First Advocacy Example

This synthetic example shows the intended posture for underwriting disclosure and claims support: proactive, customer-first, and still compliant.

## Example 1 — Underwriting / Disclosure Support

### Agent input

```text
客户三年前有过一次住院，现在已经康复。我想帮他顺利承保，但不能让他以后因为告知问题被拒赔或被指责误导。应该怎么和客户沟通？
```

### Expected assistant behavior

Produce a **disclosure support memo**, not a shortcut around disclosure.

- Required disclosure facts:
  - Past hospitalization, approximate date, diagnosis/treatment category, recovery status, follow-up status, medication status, recurrence if any, and any carrier/application questions that apply.
- Helpful accurate context:
  - Recovery evidence, stable follow-up, physician notes, normal test results, return to work, no recurrence, or other source-backed favorable facts.
- Supporting documents:
  - Approved application/fact-find form, discharge summary, follow-up reports, medication record, occupational/financial documents where relevant.
- Customer-safe language:
  - "我们要如实回答投保申请上的问题，同时把对你有利的真实资料准备齐，避免因为信息不完整让核保看起来比实际更差。不能隐瞒、淡化或改写事实，但可以把康复、复查、稳定情况和医生资料一起提交，争取公平核保。"
- Review owner:
  - Licensed agent plus compliance/underwriting support for unusual or unclear facts.

### Must not do

- Do not conceal, minimize, omit, or reframe material facts.
- Do not suggest `leave it out`, `make it sound minor`, or `do not mention`.
- Do not guarantee approval.

## Example 2 — Late Property Claim Support

### Agent input

```text
客户三年前买过家庭财产保险，当年确实发生财产损失。保单不是我卖的，是我做保单检视时发现的。客户之前甚至不知道这份保险的保障范围，是我解释后才知道可能符合理赔条件。现在看起来已经超过通常理赔时效，我应该怎么协助？
```

### Expected assistant behavior

Produce a **claim advocacy memo** and **client-interest action plan**, not a dead-end caveat.

- Known facts:
  - Policy existed; property loss occurred; customer only learned coverage/claim path during later policy review; claim timing issue exists.
- Policy/source points to verify:
  - Policy wording, notice provisions, claim limitation language, claim reporting instructions, insurer correspondence, jurisdiction-specific timing rules.
- Timeliness analysis:
  - Preserve when the customer knew or should have known they had coverage, a claimable loss, and a practical claim path. Mark legal/claims interpretation `[verify with claims/legal/compliance]`.
- Evidence to gather:
  - Policy schedule, full wording, loss photos/repair invoices, date of loss, prior communications, proof customer lacked notice/understanding of coverage, policy review notes.
- Client-interest action plan:
  1. Build a concise timeline.
  2. Prepare a document checklist.
  3. Ask the carrier/claims specialist for review, raising the `knew or should have known` timing point where appropriate.
  4. Escalate denial, complaint, or limitation-period pushback to claims/legal/compliance review.
- Customer-safe language:
  - "时间间隔确实是重点风险，不能保证结果。但我们不会只因为过了三年就直接放弃。我会先帮你核对保单和理赔条款，整理当年损失证据，并说明你是什么时候才知道这份保障和理赔路径，再提交给保险公司/理赔渠道争取审核。"

### Must not do

- Do not say nothing can be done without reviewing sources.
- Do not promise payout.
- Do not advise altering documents or inventing evidence.
- Do not provide unauthorized legal advice.

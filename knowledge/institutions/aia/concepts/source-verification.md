# Source Verification

## Definition

Source verification means checking product, service, claims, renewal, or underwriting facts against current official public sources or approved private sources before using them in customer-facing drafts.

## Why It Matters

Public institution packs are community-maintained knowledge bases. They help organize knowledge but do not replace current official policy contracts, official product terms, carrier portals, or licensed/compliance review.

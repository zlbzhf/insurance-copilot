## Questions Covered

The public FAQ summary covers recurring claim-preparation questions including:

- general submission timeline and late-submission handling;
- who receives benefit payment for common claim categories;
- valid receipt requirements and electronic receipt handling;
- return of original documents;
- balance claims after reimbursement by other insurers or parties;
- medical-claim document issues, critical-illness evidence examples, and death-claim payee / estate scenarios `[verify]`.

## Answers Summary

- Timeline: claim form and supporting documents are described as generally due within 90 days of the relevant accident, day surgery, hospital discharge, or death event; late submission should include a reason for consideration.
- Payee: medical, critical illness, accident, and disability claim payments are generally described as payable to the policyowner or estate if deceased; death claim payment depends on beneficiary, ownership, assignment, estate, and related status `[verify]`.
- Receipts: valid medical receipts should show patient name, consultation date, diagnosis, charge breakdown, and provider stamp or doctor signature.
- Other reimbursement: if another insurer or party has reimbursed part of the expense, the customer should disclose this and provide the compensation breakdown for a balance claim `[verify]`.
- Medical hospital documents: the FAQ distinguishes public hospital, private/overseas hospital, and China hospital document examples; verify the current claim type and case facts before use.

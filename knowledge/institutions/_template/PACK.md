# Institution Pack Template

This pack is a public LLM-wiki-style knowledge base for one insurer/institution.

## Scope

- Public product and service knowledge.
- Public source summaries and links.
- Publicly shareable compliance/disclaimer notes.
- Community-maintained comparisons with explicit sources and confidence.

## Not Allowed

- Customer data.
- Private agent notes.
- Unpublished internal materials.
- Confidential SOPs.
- Full copyrighted documents unless redistribution is allowed.

## Source Hierarchy

1. Official policy contract / product terms if public.
2. Official product brochure or web page.
3. Official service/claims/renewal page.
4. Public regulator material.
5. Community summary citing sources.

## Verification Rule

Treat public summaries as educational knowledge. Always verify against current official source before customer-facing or regulated use.
